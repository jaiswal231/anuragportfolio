Who I Am

Enthusiasm, Curiosity & Relentless Learning

A glimpse into my mindset, motivation, and the driving passion behind my decision to pursue Computer Science and Engineering.

My journey into computer science began with an innate desire to understand the logic behind computing systems. Instead of treating software as black magic, I want to uncover the mathematical elegance and engineering discipline that powers it.

Currently enrolled as a first-year B.Tech CSE undergraduate at Shoolini University, I spend my time understanding the foundational building blocks of computation: how data flows through memory, how algorithms solve problems in finite steps, and how clean code creates enduring software.
